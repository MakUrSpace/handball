🎮 VR HANDBALL CONTROLS

🥽 Meta Quest 3 Mixed Reality Passthrough

Click 'ENTER PASSTHROUGH (AR)' on the top bar or inside VR to see your real-world room with color cameras while playing against glowing holographic neon court boundaries!

🕶️ Meta Quest 3 Bare Hands Tracking

Use bare hands! Swing palm or fist forward to strike the ball against the front wall. Aim the palm to steer its laser and pitch the contact disk; rotate through impact to add spin. Extend the left hand to glance at the upper forearm/shoulder HUD.

🕹️ Touch Plus Controllers & Haptics

Swing either controller's contact disk into the ball for a haptic strike. To serve or reset, physically tap the controls on the left wrist with the right contact disk; selector rays and controller triggers cannot activate those wrist buttons.
